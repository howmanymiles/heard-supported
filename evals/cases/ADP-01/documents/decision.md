# Notice of determination

We have made a determination on your application for Adult Disability Payment. You are not entitled to Adult Disability Payment.

You scored 6 points for daily living activities and 4 points for mobility activities. You need at least 8 points in either to be entitled to the standard rate, and at least 12 points for the enhanced rate.

We made this determination using your application, the letter from your GP dated 3 September 2026 and the information you gave us by phone. We did not need a consultation.

## Our reasons

Washing and bathing: you told us you use a long-handled sponge, so you need an aid to wash. You manage at the sink on most days.
Engaging socially: you are able to speak with your family and with your GP, so you can engage socially with other people unaided.
Planning and following journeys: you told us you know the routes to places you go, so you can plan and follow the route of a journey unaided.
Moving around: your GP says you can walk about 150 metres before needing to stop.

## If you disagree

You can ask us to look at this determination again. This is called a re-determination. You must ask within 42 days of the day you are told about this determination. If you ask later, you need to tell us why, and you must ask within one year.
