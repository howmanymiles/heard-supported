## Your conditions

Fibromyalgia, diagnosed 2021. Anxiety and panic disorder. Low back pain.

## Washing and bathing

I can wash at the sink most days if I sit down. We only have a bath with the shower over it, there is no walk-in shower and no rails. My husband has to help me get in and out of the bath because my legs give way and I can't lift them over the side. If he is on a late shift I just wash at the sink. I use a long-handled sponge for my back.

## Mixing with other people

I don't really see anyone now apart from my sister and my husband. If I have to go to anything, like the doctor or a parents' evening, my sister comes with me and does most of the talking. She knows how my panic attacks start and she can calm me down before it gets bad. Without her I would just not go.

## Going out

I don't go out on my own. My sister phones me in the morning on the days I have somewhere to be and talks me through getting ready and out of the door. Even to the shop at the end of the road I need her to talk me into it. If she doesn't phone I usually stay in.

I know the way to places, I am not confused about routes. It is the panic that stops me.

## Moving around

I can walk to the end of the road and back, about 150 metres, then I have to rest because of the pain in my back and legs. I don't use a stick.

## Preparing food and dressing

I sit on a perching stool in the kitchen to cook. I use a long shoehorn and elastic laces because bending is painful.
