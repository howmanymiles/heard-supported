## About your health conditions or disabilities

**Please tell us about your illnesses, disabilities or conditions and how long you have had them.**

Bad back for years, it got a lot worse about 2 years ago. They call it degenerative disc disease in the bottom of my back. I get sciatica down my left leg, sometimes it goes right to my foot. I also have anxiety, the doctor says generalised anxiety. That started after I had to stop work at the warehouse.

**Medication and treatment**

Naproxen twice a day, omeprazole with it. Amitriptyline at night for the nerve pain. Sertraline in the morning. Propranolol if I need it. I'm getting physio at the hospital. I had a scan last year.

**Hospital or clinic appointments**

Physio every couple of weeks. I'm on a list for the talking therapy.
