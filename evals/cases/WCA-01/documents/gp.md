**Summary**

This gentleman has had low back pain for many years which became significantly worse in 2024 while working as a warehouse operative. MRI in June 2025 showed multilevel degenerative change, most marked at L4/5 and L5/S1, with mild left foraminal narrowing at L5/S1. He was reviewed by the orthopaedic spinal service, who did not consider him a candidate for surgery and recommended physiotherapy and pain management. He is currently attending physiotherapy at Eastvale Community Hospital.

He reports pain on prolonged sitting and standing and reduced walking tolerance. On examination in August straight leg raise was limited on the left at around 50 degrees. Power was normal.

He developed generalised anxiety following loss of his job. He describes constant worry, poor sleep and physical symptoms of anxiety. He becomes very distressed when plans change unexpectedly and his wife has noticed this. GAD-7 score was 15 in July 2026. Sertraline was increased to 100 mg in May 2026 with some benefit. He has been referred to the primary care mental health team for CBT and is on the waiting list.
