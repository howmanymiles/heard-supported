## Moving around / corner shop

Corner shop is at end of her street, client says "just past the third lamp post". Adviser walked it after appt, approx. 80 m door to door (paced, not measured).

Client describes trip: stops at next door's wall to lean and get breath, then again at the bus shelter, sometimes a third time. Takes her about 15 mins to get there. Leans on counter in the shop.

Said "most days" on form. On questioning goes more like 3 days a week. On bad days (cold, damp, chest bad) doesn't go at all.

Better days maybe 1 or 2 a week, when she can get to the shop with fewer stops.

Stick in left hand outdoors. Indoors holds furniture and wall.

## Dressing

Sits on bed to dress, takes about 20 mins with rests between items. Says socks are the hardest part. Adviser did not ask about any equipment for dressing.

## Food

## Medication

Uses spacer with both inhalers (GP gave her it). Manages own inhalers, pharmacy blister pack for BP tablet. No issues.
