Date of this letter: 2026-07-22

## Your Personal Independence Payment decision

We have looked at your claim for Personal Independence Payment (PIP) and have decided you are not entitled to PIP.

**Daily living component:** You are not entitled to the daily living component. You scored 2 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate.

**Mobility component:** You are not entitled to the mobility component. You scored 4 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate.

## How we made our decision

We looked at all the information we have, including your PIP2 form, the report from the health professional who assessed you on 16 July 2026, your GP's letter dated 12 June 2026 and the physiotherapy and occupational therapy reports.

## Reasons for our decision

You told us you can walk about 50 metres and that you walk to the corner shop most days. The health professional saw you walk from the waiting room to the assessment room with a stick without stopping. We have accepted that you need a shower seat to wash. We have not accepted that you need help or aids for your other daily living activities.

## If you disagree with this decision

You can ask us to look at this decision again. This is called a mandatory reconsideration. You must ask within one month of the date of this letter. If you ask after this, we will only look at the decision again in some circumstances.

When you contact us, tell us why you think the decision is wrong and send us any new evidence you have.
