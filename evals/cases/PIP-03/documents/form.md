# PIP2 — How your disability affects you (copy transcribed for adviser)

Client ref: HS-2003

## About your health conditions or disabilities

COPD (my lungs). Arthritis in both knees, the right one is worse. I have high blood pressure as well but the tablets keep that under control.

## Medication and treatments

Blue inhaler when I need it, purple inhaler twice a day. Blood pressure tablet. Paracetamol for my knees and sometimes the gel. I had a course of steroids and antibiotics back in November when my chest was bad.

## Q3 Preparing food

I manage most of the time. I don't do big meals anymore, it's just me in the house. I use the microwave a lot because I can't stand at the cooker for long without getting puffed out. If I'm doing potatoes I sit down to peel them.

## Q4 Eating and drinking

No problems.

## Q5 Managing treatments

I take my inhalers myself, I've been on them for years. The chemist does my tablets in a pack so that's easy.

## Q6 Washing and bathing

I've got a seat in the shower that the council put in. I sit on it to wash. I couldn't stand in the shower long enough otherwise. I don't wash my hair in the shower, I do it at the kitchen sink.

## Q7 Managing toilet needs or incontinence

No problems with the toilet itself. It's getting along the hall to it that can be the bother some days, as I get breathless.

## Q8 Dressing and undressing

I can dress myself. It takes me a while and I sit on the bed to do it.

## Q9 Communicating

No problems.

## Q10 Reading and understanding

I need my reading glasses but that's all.

## Q11 Mixing with other people

No problems, I like a chat.

## Q12 Making decisions about money

I do all my own bills and my own shopping. No problems.

## Q13 Planning and following journeys

I go out on my own. I know where I'm going. I just don't go far now.

## Q14 Moving around

I use a stick when I'm outside. I can walk about 50 metres before I need to stop and get my breath. My knees ache too, especially the right one. Some days are worse, like when it's cold or damp. I go to the corner shop most days.

## Additional information

I don't like to make a fuss. My daughter lives up north and phones me every Sunday. My neighbour's boy helps with the bins.

I used to work in a school kitchen until I was 58 but I had to stop because of my chest.
