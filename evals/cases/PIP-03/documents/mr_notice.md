## Reasons for our decision

**Dressing and undressing.** We have changed our decision on this activity. The occupational therapist gave you a long-handled shoe horn and reports that you use it every day to put on your shoes. We accept that you need to use an aid to dress. We have awarded 2 points.

**Preparing food.** You told us you sit down to peel vegetables and that you use the microwave a lot. The occupational therapist offered you a perching stool for the kitchen and you chose not to have it. We think sitting to prepare food is your preference and that you do not need an aid. You make a simple meal each day. We have not changed our decision.

**Managing toilet needs.** You told us on your PIP2 form that you have no problems with the toilet itself. The toilet frame was fitted on the day of the occupational therapist's visit. The health professional saw you get up from a chair on your own at the assessment. We have not changed our decision.

**Moving around.** The health professional saw you walk from the waiting room to the assessment room with a stick without stopping. You told us you can walk about 50 metres and that you walk to the corner shop most days. We have looked at the walk test in the physiotherapy report, but this was on one day only. We have given more weight to what you told us and what the health professional saw. We have not changed our decision.

**Other activities.** Your representative agrees with our decision on the other activities. We have not changed them.

## If you disagree with this decision

You can appeal to an independent tribunal. The tribunal is run by HM Courts and Tribunals Service (HMCTS) and is separate from DWP.

You must appeal within one month of the date of this notice. If you appeal later than this, you must explain why, and the tribunal will decide whether it can accept your appeal.

To appeal, fill in form SSCS1, which you can get from GOV.UK, and send it to HMCTS with a copy of this notice. Do not send your appeal to us.
