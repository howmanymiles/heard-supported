# Community Rehabilitation Team — Physiotherapy assessment

To: GP, Larchfield Medical Practice
Copy: patient

Re: Patient ref HS-2003

Date of assessment: 2026-05-19 (clinic)

## Background

Lady in her early sixties, lives alone in a ground-floor flat. COPD with two exacerbations this year, one requiring admission. Bilateral knee OA, right worse than left. Uses a single-point stick in the left hand outdoors; furniture-walks indoors. Patient reported today as an average day.

## Subjective

Main limitation is breathlessness, with right knee pain a close second. Estimates she can walk "about 50 metres" before stopping. Reports good and bad days related to weather and chest. No falls in the last 12 months, one near-fall on a kerb in April.

## Objective

Six-minute walk test attempted on flat clinic corridor, using her stick, at self-selected pace.

Walk abandoned at 18 metres when patient stopped due to breathlessness and right knee pain.

SpO2 93% at rest, 86% on stopping. Borg breathlessness 7/10. Heart rate 98 rising to 121.

Required a seated rest of approximately 4 minutes before SpO2 recovered to 92%. She then walked a further 12 metres before stopping again and declined to continue.

Gait: slow, antalgic on right, reduced knee flexion bilaterally. Sit to stand from standard chair with use of both arms, 2 attempts.

## Assessment

Exercise tolerance markedly reduced, limited by both breathlessness with desaturation and right knee pain. Walking distance on testing today was considerably less than her own estimate.

## Plan

1. Placed on pulmonary rehabilitation waiting list (current wait approx. 16 weeks).
2. Home exercise programme issued: seated knee strengthening, breathing control and pacing.
3. Advised on pacing and recovery positions.
4. Not suitable for a rollator at present: patient prefers her stick and feels a frame would be awkward in her flat. To review.
5. OT home visit requested (see enclosed report).

Physiotherapist, Community Rehabilitation Team
