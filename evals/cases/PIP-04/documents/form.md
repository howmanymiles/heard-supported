# PIP2 — How your disability affects you (filled in by claimant's partner, read back to claimant)

Client ref: HS-2004

## About your health conditions or disabilities

Retinitis pigmentosa. I am registered blind (severely sight impaired). My sight has got a lot worse in the last two years. I have a bit of central vision left but it is like looking through a straw and it is blurry.

## Medication and treatments

Two lots of eye drops. Vitamin tablets.

## Q3 Preparing food

My partner does most of the cooking now. I can make a sandwich or cereal. I use the microwave with the bumps on the buttons. I don't use the hob much since I burnt myself.

## Q4 Eating and drinking

I'm fine. Sometimes I knock my drink over.

## Q5 Managing treatments

I do my eye drops myself.

## Q6 Washing and bathing

No problems.

## Q7 Managing toilet needs or incontinence

No problems.

## Q8 Dressing and undressing

I can get dressed myself. I keep my clothes organised so I know what's what. My partner sometimes tells me if something doesn't go or if I've got a mark on my top.

## Q9 Communicating

No problems talking or hearing.

## Q10 Reading and understanding

I can't read letters or anything printed. I can't read signs when I'm out. My partner reads my post to me.

## Q11 Mixing with other people

I'm fine with people. It's harder in a group because I can't see who is talking to me.

## Q12 Making decisions about money

My partner reads me the bills but I decide what we pay and when. I do my own banking on my phone with the talking setting.

## Q13 Planning and following journeys

I use my long cane. I have a guide dog, she is 3, and I use her for getting to work and back. I'm OK on routes I know. If it's somewhere new I need my partner or a friend to come with me, or I take the dog if someone has walked it with us first.

## Q14 Moving around

No problems with walking.

## Additional information

I work part time (3 days) as a call handler. I get to work on the bus with my guide dog. I had to give up driving 8 years ago.
