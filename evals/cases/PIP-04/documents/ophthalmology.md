## Plan

1. Continue dorzolamide 2% three times daily to both eyes. I have stressed the importance of using these regularly, as stopping them is likely to allow the macular oedema to worsen.
2. Lubricants as needed.
3. Review in 6 months with OCT.

She mentioned that the two drop bottles feel similar and she has on occasion used the wrong one. I have asked the eye clinic liaison officer to arrange tactile labels.

Glare: tinted filter lenses discussed; she will trial these through the low vision service.

She remains certified severely sight impaired.

Yours sincerely

Consultant Ophthalmologist, Medical Retina
