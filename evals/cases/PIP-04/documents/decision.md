## Your Personal Independence Payment decision

We have looked at your claim for Personal Independence Payment (PIP) and have decided you are not entitled to PIP.

**Daily living component:** You are not entitled to the daily living component. You scored 2 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate.

**Mobility component:** You are not entitled to the mobility component. You scored 0 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate.

## How we made our decision

We looked at all the information we have, including your PIP2 form, the report from the health professional who assessed you by telephone on 9 October 2026, your GP's letter dated 8 September 2026 and the letter from the hospital eye service dated 30 July 2026.

## Reasons for our decision

We accept that your sight loss affects you and that you use aids in the kitchen. You told us you travel to work by bus and go to the local shops, and that you read text messages on your phone. You do not have a physical condition that limits your walking. We have not accepted that you need help from another person for your daily living or mobility activities.

## If you disagree with this decision

You can ask us to look at this decision again. This is called a mandatory reconsideration. You must ask within one month of the date of this letter. If you ask after this, we will only look at the decision again in some circumstances.

When you contact us, tell us why you think the decision is wrong and send us any new evidence you have.
