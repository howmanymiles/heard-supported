# Adviser interview notes

Client ref: HS-2004
Date: 2026-10-07
Location: advice centre, client came by bus with guide dog. Partner joined last 30 mins.
Client asked for an audio summary of the notes rather than large print.

## Background

30s, lives with partner in a 2-bed flat. RP, certified SSI 2024. Works part-time 3 days a week, call centre, uses screen reader at work. Guide dog since 2025, trained by a guide dog charity. Gave up driving 8 yrs ago. Docs: PIP2 copy, GP summary, eye clinic letter + CVI summary.

## Reading

Can't read any print without magnifier. Uses electronic magnifier at home for short things like a label or an appointment date, has to hold it very close and it takes a long time. Tried a letter from the council with it: "gave up after a couple of lines".

Can't use magnifier for signs, bus numbers, shop prices. Uses phone app that reads text aloud (camera) for some letters but says it gets lots wrong.

Partner reads all post and bills to her, every week.

## Money

Partner reads out bills; client decides what to pay and when, does phone banking with VoiceOver. No difficulty with the decisions themselves, issue is reading the paperwork. Counts change by feel / phone app for notes.

## Food

Burnt her forearm on oven shelf approx. 18 months ago, needed dressing at walk-in centre. Since then doesn't use oven or hob alone. Cut finger peeling veg earlier this year, "steri-strips", now doesn't use the sharp knife. Partner cooks evening meal every day. When partner is on late shift she has microwave meal or sandwich.

Rehab worker did kitchen work 2024 (bump-ons on microwave, liquid level indicator). Liquid level indicator: "I use it for hot drinks". Hasn't had a kitchen review since sight got worse.

## Eye drops

Two bottles feel the same. Has put the lubricant in instead of the dorzolamide "a few times". Eye clinic liaison officer sent tactile labels 3 wks ago; partner stuck them on. Client now feels the label on each bottle every time before putting drops in. Says she can do the drops herself as long as the labels are on.

## Dressing

Has a colour detector from rehab worker; client mentioned it in passing, adviser didn't ask how often she uses it or whether it works.

Partner (joined later): "sometimes" tells her a top has a stain or the colours clash before she goes to work. Didn't say how often. Client says she gets dressed herself and isn't bothered about matching at home.

## Journeys

Long cane used outdoors "nearly always". Guide dog for the commute (bus) and to the local shops: routes the dog has been trained on.

New places: needs partner or friend to go with her, or someone walks the route with her and the dog first. Example: hospital eye clinic moved building this year, partner went with her each time. Has got lost once trying a new route alone with cane (missed stop, ended up on a dual carriageway slip road, phoned partner).

Familiar routes: manages with cane or dog. Adviser asked if she ever goes out without either: says only to partner's mum two doors along, "I just follow the wall". Did not ask whether she could manage other familiar routes without cane.

## Walking

No physical issues. Broke a bone in foot last year after tripping on kerb, healed. Walks dog daily.

## People

Fine one to one. Group situations at work harder, can't see faces or who's speaking, misses cues. No anxiety. Partner says she's "the sociable one".

## Other

No issues with washing, toilet, talking/hearing. Mood ok. Mentioned glare is worse in winter sun.
