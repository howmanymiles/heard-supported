# Your Personal Independence Payment decision

We have looked at your claim for Personal Independence Payment (PIP). You are entitled to the standard rate of the mobility part from the date of your claim. You are not entitled to the daily living part.

## Daily living part

You are not entitled to the daily living part. You scored 0 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate and at least 12 points to get the enhanced rate.

## Mobility part

You are entitled to the standard rate. You scored 10 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate and at least 12 points to get the enhanced rate.

Your award is until 13 June 2028. We will contact you before then to review your award.

## What we used to make our decision

- Your PIP2 form dated 28 September 2026
- Letter from your GP dated 3 October 2026
- Letter from your community psychiatric nurse dated 10 October 2026
- The report from the health professional who assessed you face to face on 26 November 2026

## Reasons

You told us you have depression and social anxiety. At your assessment you engaged well with the health professional and your self-care was adequate. Your mother encourages you at home, but we have decided you are able to carry out the daily living activities without help. You attended your assessment with your mother. We accept you need another person with you to follow an unfamiliar journey, so we have awarded 10 points for activity 11.

## If you disagree with this decision

You can ask us to look at this decision again. This is called a mandatory reconsideration. You must ask us within one month of the date of this letter. If you ask later than this, you must tell us why. Tell us which parts of the decision you disagree with and send any new evidence you have.
