Dear Adviser,

I have been this young woman's CPN since February 2025 and visit her at home every two weeks. She has not been able to attend the CMHT base for appointments; on the one occasion she tried, in March 2025, she left the waiting room before being seen and was found by her mother in the car park, tearful and shaking.

Her mood remains low most of the time. She reports poor motivation, poor sleep, and spending much of the day in her bedroom. Her PHQ-9 score at my last visit was 22 and her GAD-7 was 17. She has some days where she manages more, but these are in the minority.

Daily living

Self-care is significantly affected by low motivation. Her mother tells me that she will not shower unless prompted, and that without prompting she has gone over a week without washing. With prompting she showers about twice a week. I have seen her on visits in the same clothes as the previous visit on several occasions.

She does not prepare meals for herself. When her mother is at work she frequently does not eat until her mother returns. When her mother prompts and stays in the kitchen with her she will help with simple tasks.

Medication: before her mother started reminding her each morning (around April 2025) she was missing doses most days, and her mood deteriorated. Adherence has been good since the reminders started. Her mother keeps the propranolol and hands it to her when she becomes panicky.

Engaging with others and going out

Contact with people outside the household causes marked anxiety: palpitations, nausea, shaking and an urge to escape. She can engage with me now, after a long period of building trust, but only with her mother present for the first part of each visit. She does not engage with unfamiliar professionals without her mother, who has supported her through all of her appointments and knows how to calm her.

She does not leave the house alone. Journeys happen only with her mother, by car. When she has attempted short journeys on her own as part of graded exposure work, she has turned back within a few minutes each time.

She is physically well and I have no concerns about her mobility, speech or understanding.
