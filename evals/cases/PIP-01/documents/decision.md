# Your Personal Independence Payment decision

We have looked at your claim for Personal Independence Payment (PIP). You are not entitled to PIP from the date of your claim.

## Daily living part

You are not entitled to the daily living part. You scored 2 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate and at least 12 points to get the enhanced rate.

## Mobility part

You are not entitled to the mobility part. You scored 0 points. You need at least 8 points to get the standard rate and at least 12 points to get the enhanced rate.

## Reasons

You told us your tiredness varies and that you have bad days. The evidence from your MS nurse shows bad days on around 4 days in 10. We must decide which descriptor applies to you on more than half of the days. On most days you are able to prepare food, dress and walk more than 200 metres. You need a shower stool to wash, so we have awarded 2 points for activity 4.

## If you disagree with this decision

You can ask us to look at this decision again. This is called a mandatory reconsideration. You must ask us within one month of the date of this letter. If you ask later than this, you must tell us why. Tell us which parts of the decision you disagree with and send any new evidence you have.
